20 open-weight families: a comparison matrix

This table does one thing: put parameters, context, licence, local feasibility and approximate VRAM on one screen so you do not have to open 20 family cards. Figures come from the cards and official model cards; VRAM is a rough 4-bit weight estimate and excludes KV cache, vision encoders and concurrency.

“Single GPU” means the smallest usable tier fits a 24–48 GB consumer card or one 80 GB card; “multi-GPU” means even that exceeds one card. Licences follow the official model card; the table only groups them.

Licences are more than open/closed: commercial use, attribution and MAU thresholds are broken out in the licence checker.

Command A Command A+
2026-05		 Flagship 218B MoE (25B active; multi-GPU / large VRAM); Previous r7b (7B-class, single card) 128K Apache-2.0 Multi-GPU / 120 GB+ Cohere's enterprise agentic line: A+ opened up under Apache-2.0 (the previous generation was gated), pitched at enterprise and multilingual work. At 218B scale the local-deployment bar is high. This site carries no quantisation comparison (GGUF ecosystem too thin — see below).
DeepSeek V4.1-Flash
2026-09		 Single MoE tier: 552B backbone plus a 196B Engram conditional-memory table and a vision tower (the official card's components add up to roughly 763B total; the README gives no single total), local runability depends on quantisation and memory See model card MIT Cluster / 400 GB+ MIT-licensed with a distinctive KV-cache-compression roadmap; an active quantisation ecosystem (antirez, author of Redis and a llama.cpp ecosystem participant, maintains a quantised build; recorded on the series page). For deployers who track the frontier and can host large models.
Falcon Falcon H1R 7B
2026-01		 7B (single GPU); Previous multi-size line (see official page) See model card Falcon TII Single GPU / 6 GB TII’s hybrid-architecture family: H1R 7B uses a Transformer-Mamba mix to keep 7B runnable on one GPU, suited to long-sequence and low-VRAM experiments. The licence is not OSI, so read it before commercial use; the ecosystem is smaller than Llama or Qwen.
Gemma Gemma 4
2026-03		 On-device E2B / E4B: phones and browsers; Laptop 12B / 26B-A4B; Server 31B (dense) 128K (E series: see model card) Apache-2.0 From 2 GB on-device; ~18 GB for 31B The family with the widest size spread (E2B to 31B), one line from edge to server. Licensing is Apache-2.0 plus a use policy — read the policy text before download. This site's gemma-4 series covers three of the tiers.
GLM (Z.ai) GLM-5.3
2026-08		 Flash tier: MIT-licensed, the first choice for local deployment; Flagship 5.3: 754B MoE, server-class; licence per the release page See model card MIT / Vendor licence Flash ~180 GB; 5.3 cluster Strong at Chinese agentic work and code, with a very fast iteration cadence. Flagship and Flash tiers carry different licences — for local deployment, prefer the MIT Flash tier. This site's glm-5 series tracks GLM-5.3.
gpt-oss gpt-oss
2025-08		 Local 20B (21B total / 3.6B active, within 16GB); Single-GPU server 120B (117B total / 5.1B active, 80GB card) — Apache-2.0 20B ~12–16 GB; 120B ~70 GB OpenAI's first open-weight MoE line: Apache-2.0, designed to ship quantized (MXFP4) from the start, single-GPU operation is its headline scenario. This site covers the gpt-oss quantisation comparison.
Granite Granite 4.2
2026-11		 3B (edge / single machine); 8B (single GPU); 30B (multi-GPU / enterprise) 128K Apache-2.0 3B ~2 GB; 8B ~5 GB; 30B ~18 GB IBM’s enterprise-oriented open-weight family: restrained sizes, clean licensing, and an emphasis on workflows and coding, suited to private deployment in compliance-heavy enterprises. It is not a benchmark-maximalist line; its advantage is licensing, auditability and enterprise integration.
KAT Coder KAT-Coder-V2.5
2026-07		 Code-focused 35B-A3B (35B total / 3B active) See model card Apache-2.0 ~20 GB Kuaishou Kwaipilot's coding line: the API build stays closed, the Dev build is open-weight. It is this site's representative "derived model" — the vendor itself declares Qwen3.6-35B-A3B as its base, so choosing it means choosing the Qwen lineage plus Kuaishou's code post-training.
Kimi Kimi K3.1 Pro
2026-10		 Flagship 2.8T MoE (K3.1 Pro, 104B active, 1M context); Flagship 2.8T MoE (K3; active count not itemised officially); Branch builds 7B–72B (Kimi-Audio / Kimi-Dev etc.) 1M Vendor licence / Vendor licence Cluster / 1.4 TB+ The domestic open-weight flagship: K3.1 Pro pushes K3 post-training to a new tier — 2.8T total / 104B active, 1M context — with weights opened on 2026-11-04 under a custom Kimi K3.1 Pro License. The Kimi chat product sits in the CAC first batch; weights and service filing are separate.
Laguna Laguna 2.1 / XS.2
2026-08		 Local XS (33B total / 3B active; the vendor says a 36GB Mac runs it); Larger S / M builds (official org) 262K (XS) OpenMDW-1.1 XS ~20 GB; vendor says it runs on a 36 GB Mac The most permissively licensed coding line (OpenMDW-1.1, fully permissive); its pitch is agentic coding on a local machine, with 3B active parameters as the source of that locality. This site covers Laguna XS-2.1 quantisations.
Llama Llama 4
2025-04		 Small 1B / 3B: on-device and vision-small; Medium 8B / 70B: the common local-deployment tiers; Large 405B / 17B-128E MoE: server-class See model card Vendor licence 8B ~6 GB; 70B ~40 GB The largest open-weight ecosystem by far — fine-tunes, quantisations and tooling are everywhere — but the licence is a community licence with attribution ("Built with Llama") and scale (700M MAU) clauses. Read it fully before commercial use.
MiniCPM MiniCPM5
2026-08		 On-device 1B / 2B (MiniCPM5-1B / 2B, dense); Previous 4B–8B (MiniCPM3/4 line) See model card Apache-2.0 1B–2B ~1–2 GB The domestic on-device small-model family: small sizes plus fully open training data — a Chinese family that gives you for the "train one yourself" route. Alongside Zhipu, another filed domestic line (MiniCPM filing number Beijing-MiniCPM-202503180058).
Mistral Mistral Large 4
2026-10		 Flagship tier: 1.05T total / 49B active (52B including embeddings and output); Small 24B-119B (MoE): the locally runnable open tier; Medium 128B: flagship dense tier 1M (Large 4) Mistral Ecosystem / Apache-2.0 / Vendor licence Large 4 cluster; Small 24B ~15 GB A European open-weight flagship: 1T-class MoE, 1M context and vision input, under a modified MIT licence (separate MaaS grant needed). For deployers who need open weights, a European compliance story and multilingual coverage; local hosting is extremely demanding, and the official release includes an NVFP4 checkpoint and a DSpark speculative-decoding draft head.
Muse Muse Glimmer
2026-08		 30B dense (Glimmer tier) See model card Apache-2.0 30B ~18 GB Meta's newer open-weight line beside Llama (the meta-models org). Publication is gated: sign in and consent on HF before download — a hard constraint when choosing, stated plainly on the card. This site covers Muse Glimmer 30B quantisations.
Nemotron Nemotron 3.5
2026-11		 30B-A3B (single machine / low latency); 120B-A12B (multi-GPU); 550B-A55B (cluster) 1M NVIDIA Open Model 30B-A3B ~18–20 GB; 120B ~70 GB NVIDIA’s open-weight agent family: three MoE tiers from a single machine to a cluster, pitched at long context, reasoning and local agents. Its value is not “another open model” but the coupling with NVIDIA’s inference stack, NIM and hardware; without NVIDIA hardware the benefit is smaller.
OLMo OLMo 3.1
2026-11		 7B (single GPU); 32B (multi-GPU) 65K Apache-2.0 7B ~5 GB; 32B ~20 GB Ai2’s fully open research family: it releases data, code, checkpoints and the training recipe together, suited to reproducible experiments and teaching. Its advantage is not a leaderboard score but end-to-end inspectability; the cost is that you bear the training and evaluation burden.
Ornith Ornith 1.5
2026-08		 Single-card 9B (dense); Workhorse 35B-A3B (35B total / ~3B active); Flagship 397B See model card MIT 9B ~6 GB; 35B ~22 GB A newcomer (only two generations so far, both 2026): MIT licence plus three tiers from single-card to flagship. The publisher DeepReinforce's jurisdiction of registration is not officially stated; this site treats it as foreign-made and says so in the origin note (S52 convention).
Phi Phi-4-reasoning line
2026-08		 On-device mini 3.8B; Single-card 14B–15B (phi-4 dense / reasoning-vision 15B) See model card MIT 15B ~10 GB Microsoft's small-but-high-quality line: MIT, sizes concentrated at 4B–15B, and the vendor's pitch is "teach small models with synthetic data". The current line is reasoning-vision (still a phi-4 post-train on the weight lineage). This site carries no quantisation comparison — the current generation's GGUF ecosystem is too thin (see below).
Qwen Qwen4-Max
2026-11		 Closed Max 2.4T: Qwen4-Max, 71B active, 1M context (API flagship); Small 0.6B-9B: on-device, free tiers, CPU-capable; Medium 14B-35B: the local-deployment workhorse (27B dense leads this generation); Large 180B-2.4T: MoE flagships needing server-class VRAM 1M (Max / Qwen4) Apache-2.0 / Vendor licence 27B ~16–18 GB; Max cluster One of the strongest open-weight families for Chinese and code. Qwen4-Max is the closed-API flagship; the open-weight line remains Qwen3.8, and licence and hardware floor should be read separately.
Tencent Hunyuan Hy4 / Hy4 flash
2026-11		 Hy4: 770B total / 49B active (HF model card), 1M context; Hy4 flash: 224B total (64B Engram included, 12B active; model-card components sum to about 220B), 1M context; Hy4 Preview: 770B total / 49B active (comparison figure in the Hy4 flash release); Hy3: 295B total (MoE, 21B active), 256K context. All under the Tencent Hy License, not an OSI-standard licence. 1M (Hy4 flash) Vendor licence Hy4 flash ~120 GB+ An uncommon large-total/high-active Chinese open-weight family: production Hy4 is 770B total / 49B active, targeting visual understanding, long context and long-horizon agents, while Hy4 flash trades 224B total / 12B active for speed and cost. Weights opened on 2026-11-20 under the Tencent Hy License, not an OSI-standard licence; there is no GGUF coverage yet — read the licence text and the hardware floor before choosing.
Thinking Machines · Inkling Inkling 2 Small
2026-11		 Flagship 594B-A17B: Inkling 2 Small, multimodal, 1M context; the MXFP8 checkpoint needs about 800GB of aggregate VRAM, not a single-machine local tier.; Local 26.4B-A3.4B: Inkling 2 Mini, 24-layer looped MoE, Q4_K_M at 16.61GB, runnable on 24GB-class cards at short-to-medium context. — Apache-2.0 — Two lines in the same generation: Small is the 594B-class flagship for frontier agent and multimodal work; Mini is the 26B-class local node that trades repeated layers for capacity. Both are Apache-2.0 with 1M context, but their hardware floors differ by an order of magnitude and they do not replace each other.

There is no “best” family, only whether a tier fits, whether the licence works and whether quantisations are maintained. For the selection order see models home and family cards.
