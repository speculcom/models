Large-scale image-text pairs for contrastive learning and image-text retrieval research.

COCO Captions

A standard captioning baseline with stable annotation format, suitable for small controlled comparisons.

Size
About 330k images / 5 captions each
Licence
Annotations CC-BY-4.0; images follow Flickr sources

LLaVA-Instruct-150K

GPT-generated image-text instructions covering description, reasoning and dialogue; an early multimodal SFT baseline.

Size
150k image-text instructions
Licence
LLaVA data terms; images follow COCO

Hugging Face direct download
