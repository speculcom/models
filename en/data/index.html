Data and training corpora

Datasets are not automatically better when bigger. Start with the licence and commercial boundary, then the scale definition, language, quality and task fit. 40 dataset cards in 8 categories; each states what it can and cannot do.

Before use, check three things: the licence and commercial boundary; the scale definition (tokens / samples / raw pages); and remove evaluation sets from training data. Cards are an index and risk note, not legal advice.
