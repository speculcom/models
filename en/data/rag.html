RAG corpora

Evidence libraries for retrieval QA; quality, freshness and permissions matter more than chunk size. This page lists 5 dataset cards; open a card for licence, size, task fit and use boundaries.

RAG corpora

Natural Questions

Real search queries with Wikipedia passage annotations, useful for joint retrieval and reading evaluation.

Size
About 307k real queries
Licence
CC-BY-SA-3.0
Access
Direct download

Wikipedia pages change and old annotations may not align. Fix a snapshot for RAG evaluation.

RAG corpora

HotpotQA

Questions require evidence from two or more passages, useful for testing retrieval recall and evidence stitching.

Size
About 113k multi-hop questions
Licence
CC-BY-4.0
Access
Direct download

Some questions depend on Wikipedia structure. It does not cover noise and permissions in a real document store.

RAG corpora

BEIR

A cross-domain retrieval benchmark for comparing embedding and retrieval generalisation.

Size
18 retrieval datasets
Licence
Apache-2.0
Access
Direct download

Subsets have different licences and domains. Do not average all 18 scores into one number.

RAG corpora

MS MARCO

Large-scale real search queries and passages for training and evaluating retrievers and rerankers.

Size
About 8.8M passages / 1M+ queries
Licence
MS MARCO data terms
Access
Hugging Face / official download

Queries and passages skew English web content; answers are not always complete and are not direct generation ground truth.

TriviaQA

Trivia questions with evidence documents and answer aliases, useful for retrieval-plus-reading evaluation.

Size
About 95K question-answer pairs
Licence
Apache-2.0
Access
Hugging Face direct download

Questions and evidence skew English Wikipedia and web pages; some answers have aliases and time-sensitive facts.
