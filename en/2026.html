Models · 2026 additions · 2026-11-26

Sixteen key nodes rewrote the 2026 selection table

Only 2026 models that change selection: GLM-5.4, Tencent Hy4, Inkling 2 Small, Inkling 2 Mini, Mistral Large 4, Muse Spark, MiMo-V2.6, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, GLM-5.3 and Nous Arete-27B, plus the Kimi K3 date correction. Five late entries are added: LongCat Avatar 1.6, MAGI-2, Seedance 2.5 Flash, Ling-3.2-air and ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview. Each states specs, licence and what it does not claim.

Parameters, context and licence come from official model cards; capability scores are third-party indices and labelled. This site does not rank models or treat vendor claims as measurement. GLM-5.4 is a closed release with no weights yet and is labelled as such; one quantisation result does not get its own model card: NariQ compresses Qwen3.8-27B to 3.0 / 2.5 bpw (10.1 / 8.4GB) and stays level with BF16 on DeepSWE and Terminal Bench; 2.0bpw degrades. See the papers site.

2026-11-23Closed API / frontier

GLM-5.4: the first Loop Transformer flagship

Z.ai runs 28 layers of its 750B base through two loops, trading repeated compute for reasoning depth without adding parameters; it claims intelligence in the 2–3T class. Native multimodality and “generalised coding” are the pitch, extending beyond code to video, game development and 3D modelling. Announced 2026-11-23; the API and open weights are not out yet.

Why it matters

It represents a 2026 route: instead of pushing parameters to 2T, loop the same layers and make compute depth a new scaling axis. For teams watching inference cost, long-horizon agents and multimodal coding, it is a required comparison point — but it is an announcement, not deployable weights.

Boundary / what it does not claim

“2–3T class” is a vendor claim with no published benchmark scores; looping twice means roughly double the compute at the same parameter count, so 750B is not 750B of compute cost. It is not open weights and cannot be hosted locally; neither API nor weights are available, and announcement time is not availability time. It does not claim looping always beats a wider model, or that 750B already exceeds a 2T model.

2026-11-20Open weights / frontier

Tencent Hy4: 770B / 49B active, weights open

Hy4 was announced 2026-11-04 and open-sourced 2026-11-20: a 770B-total, 49B-active MoE with over one million tokens of context and first-time visual understanding; weights are under the Tencent Hy License (not an OSI-standard licence) on GitHub, Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitCode, so read the licence text before commercial use. The same batch includes Hy4 flash: 224B total (including 64B Engram), 12B active, multimodal input and 1M context.

Why it matters

It puts 770B-class capacity, 1M context and visual input into open weights under the Tencent Hy License, and this is not a preview: Hy4 preview shipped in August, while this release adds the production weights and system card. For Chinese teams that need a self-hosted flagship and will read the licence clause by clause, Hy4 and Hy4 flash form flagship / value tiers, with flash’s 12B activation better suited to cost-sensitive deployment.

Boundary / what it does not claim

770B total means server-class weight loading and inference; 49B is active per token, not total compute, and 1M is a ceiling with a separate KV-cache cost. The production model card fills in the full parameter table, but third-party evaluations remain thinner than the preview material; vendor claims cannot replace your own task evaluation. The Tencent Hy License permits commercial use within its terms but is not Apache-2.0; the system card’s safety boundaries and prohibited uses still apply. It is not a small model, nor a homogeneous scale-up of Hy4 flash.

2026-11-20Open weights / local

Inkling 2 Mini: 26.4B MoE / 3.4B active

Thinking Machines Lab’s local multimodal node: 26.4B total, 3.4B active (about 5.4B effective through looping), a 24-layer looped MoE, 1M context, text + image input under Apache-2.0. The official checkpoint is about 20GB (NVFP4 + MXFP8); Unsloth ships 7.76–22.87GB GGUF files, with Q4_K_M at 16.61GB.

Why it matters

It puts 26B-class open weights, 1M context and native multimodality onto 24GB-class local hardware; Q4_K_M at 16.61GB sits in the same file-size band as Arete’s 17.20GB Q4, making it the most direct local comparison of 2026. The looped layers trade repeated compute for capacity; the advantage concentrates on coding and agents, not across-the-board dominance.

Boundary / what it does not claim

Not a 17B: 26.4B is total, 3.4B is active per token and 5.4B is effective active after looping; 17GB is a Q4 file size, not a parameter count. 1M is a ceiling and the KV cache consumes memory; Q4_K_M is not lossless. The vendor recommends 32GB+; 24GB with 128K is a community calculation, not a vendor guarantee. Scores come from public guides / vendor summaries and are not independently reproduced. It is not Nous Arete; Arete’s card lists “Inkling” as teacher without a version, so Mini cannot be assumed to be that exact teacher.

2026-11-06Open weights / fine-tune

Nous Arete-27B: an Apache-2.0 retune of Qwen3.8-27B

Not a new architecture, and not a “17B”: a 27B dense SFT of Qwen3.8-27B with a frozen vision encoder, trained on roughly 52.8M tokens at 64k context. Weights, evaluation and training data are public under Apache-2.0; community GGUF builds appeared quickly.

Why it matters

It extends the Qwen3.8-27B local quantisation ecosystem to a retuned variant: teams that want Apache-2.0 plus a different value alignment now have a self-hostable 27B option. Q4_K_M is 17.20GB and fits a 24GB card; 16GB cards need a shorter context or partial offload.

Boundary / what it does not claim

It is a 27B dense retune, not a 17B small model and not a new architecture. 64k is the training context; do not treat the base model’s 262k as validated here. The vendor evaluation is not independently reproduced; a change in sensitive-topic framing is not a general capability gain. Community quant quality varies, and Q4 still loses precision.

2026-11-05Open weights / frontier

Inkling 2 Small: 594B MoE / 17B active

Thinking Machines Lab’s 2026 open-weight flagship: 594B total, 17B active per token, 48 decoder-only layers, 384 experts with 8 active, 1M context, text + image input, Apache-2.0. Announced 2026-11-05, weights opened on Hugging Face 11-12; official NVFP4 and MXFP8 checkpoints, with MXFP8 requiring 800GB aggregate VRAM — not a single-machine local model.

Why it matters

It pushes the highest-scoring open weights outside China into the 594B class while combining 1M context, native image input and Apache-2.0; for frontier agents, long-horizon coding and multimodal research, it is a 2026 comparison node that cannot be skipped. Its AA score of 31 sits in the same band as Qwen3.8-27B’s public result, but its parameter and hardware floors are two orders of magnitude higher, so capability and deployability must be compared separately.

Boundary / what it does not claim

“Small” is relative to the previous generation, not a small model: 594B total and an 800GB-class MXFP8 footprint mean ordinary workstations cannot host it. AA 31 is one composite index; 86.3 / 81.7 / 13.1 come from official and media summaries and were not independently reproduced, while the 13.1% Terminal-Bench 4.0 result is a reminder that it is not a short-horizon terminal specialist. 1M is a ceiling with a separate KV-cache cost; it is neither a scaled-up Mini nor Nous Arete.

MAGI-2: a 480B video model with 30-second 1080p native audio-video

Sand.ai launched a 480B flagship video model: 480B total, about 28B active, roughly 4T pre-training tokens. It generates up to 30 seconds of 1080p 25 fps native audio-video, combines up to 20 references (10 images, 5 videos and 5 audio clips), supports six aspect ratios, timeline control and video editing. Its four-task average Elo on AA Video Arena is 1150.3, 151.8 points above Preview.
