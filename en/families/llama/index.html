What it fits / what it does not

Start here if

The largest open-weight ecosystem by far — fine-tunes, quantisations and tooling are everywhere — but the licence is a community licence with attribution ("Built with Llama") and scale (700M MAU) clauses. Read it fully before commercial use.

Rule it out first if

要跑最大档（大杯 405B / 17B-128E MoE：服务器级），显存和预算先按这一档估；小机器别拿最小档的体验代替。 许可含“Llama 4 Community License（license_name: llama4）——需登录接受条款；HF 页可逐条读许可全文”，商用或再分发前先读原文；本页不替你判断。
