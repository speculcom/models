NVIDIA　·　current generation Nemotron 3.5（2026-11）

Nemotronmodel family lineage card

NVIDIA’s open-weight agent family: three MoE tiers from a single machine to a cluster, pitched at long context, reasoning and local agents. Its value is not “another open model” but the coupling with NVIDIA’s inference stack, NIM and hardware; without NVIDIA hardware the benefit is smaller.

No Nemotron quantisation comparison yet: the three tiers span very different sizes and the GGUF ecosystem is still early, below this site’s ~300k-download threshold.

Generations

  • Nemotron 3.5 Lightning 30B-A3B2026-11

    Lightweight tier: 30B total / 3B active MoE, aimed at single-machine and low-latency agents; the official page states a 1M context.

    official page ↗
  • Nemotron 3.5 Super 120B-A12B2026-11

    Mid tier: 120B total / 12B active, suited to multi-GPU inference and more complex agent workflows.

    official page ↗
  • Nemotron 3.5 Ultra 550B-A55B2026-11

    Flagship: 550B total / 55B active, aimed at large clusters and the highest-quality tier; the local-deployment bar is very high.

    official page ↗
