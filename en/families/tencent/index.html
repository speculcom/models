Tencent　·　current generation Hy4 / Hy4 flash（2026-11）

Tencent Hunyuanmodel family lineage card

An uncommon large-total/high-active Chinese open-weight family: production Hy4 is 770B total / 49B active, targeting visual understanding, long context and long-horizon agents, while Hy4 flash trades 224B total / 12B active for speed and cost. Generations

  • Hy42026-11-20

    Announced 2026-11-04 and open-sourced 2026-11-20: a 770B-total / 49B-active MoE with a 1M-token context and first-time visual understanding; weights shipped under the Tencent Hy License on GitHub, Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitCode with a system card.

    official page ↗
  • Hy4 flash2026-11-18

    Released 2026-11-16 and open-sourced 2026-11-18: 224B total parameters (including 64B Engram), 12B active, multimodal input and 1M context; pitched at high speed and low cost, with Hy4 flash / FP8 / Base in the open release.

    official page ↗
  • Hy4 preview2026-08

    Previous preview: a 770B-total MoE with 49B active parameters and beyond one million tokens of context, positioned for long-horizon agents, coding and office work; preview and FP8 weights opened on 2026-08-28.

    official page ↗
  • Hy3 / Hy3 preview2026-07

    Hy3 launched officially in July 2026; the preview was released and open-sourced on 2026-04-23: 295B total, 21B active, up to 256K context, a hybrid fast/slow-thinking MoE.

    official page ↗

Size tiers

  • Hy4: 770B total / 49B active (HF model card; plus 1.23B vision), 1M context
  • Hy4 flash: 224B total (64B Engram included, 12B active), 1M context
  • Hy4 Preview: 770B total / 49B active (comparison figure in the Hy4 flash release)
  • Hy3: 295B total (MoE, 21B active), 256K context
