What it fits / what it does not

Start here if

The domestic open-weight flagship: K3.1 Pro pushes K3 post-training to a new tier — 2.8T total / 104B active, 1M context — with weights opened on 2026-11-04 under a custom Kimi K3.1 Pro License. The Kimi chat product sits in the CAC first batch; weights and service filing are separate.

Rule it out first if

要跑最大档（支线 7B–72B（Kimi-Audio / Kimi-Dev 等）），显存和预算先按这一档估；小机器别拿最小档的体验代替。 许可含“Kimi K3 License（模型卡 license_name: "kimi-k3"，非 OSI 标准许可，下载前读原文）”，商用或再分发前先读原文；本页不替你判断。

Generations

  • Kimi K3.1 Pro2026-10current

    Post-trained from K3 (launched 2026-10-23, weights opened 2026-11-04): a 2.8T-parameter MoE with 104B active, 1M-token context and native multimodality; the vendor reports clear gains in coding, 3D creation, financial research and long-horizon agents, with a Highspeed variant.

    official page ↗
  • Kimi K32026-07

    Current generation (open-weights day 2026-07-27). Official README: a 2.8T-parameter MoE (Stable LatentMoE, 16 of 896 experts active), KDA + AttnRes architecture, native multimodality (text/image/video), 1M-token context; "world's first open 3T-class model" is vendor-run wording.

    official page ↗
  • Kimi K2 line2025–2026

    Previous line: K2 / K2-Instruct / K2-Thinking / K2.5 / K2.6 / K2.7-Code listed in the official org (from July 2025).

    official page ↗
  • Kimi-VL / Kimi-Linear2025

    Parallel branches: the vision-language Kimi-VL-A3B line and the linear-attention Kimi-Linear-48B-A3B (official org).

    official page ↗

Official capability claims (with sources)

  • Official model card: Kimi K3.1 Pro is an "open-weight, natively multimodal agentic model" with 2.8T total parameters, 104B active and a 1,048,576-token context; the first K3.1 model, post-trained from K3.source ↗
  • Official README: "a 2.8T-parameter model built on Kimi Delta Attention (KDA) and Attention Residuals (AttnRes), with native vision capabilities and a 1-million-token context window"; "activates 16 out of 896 experts — yielding an approximate 2.5× improvement in overall scaling efficiency over Kimi K2" (the 2.5× figure is vendor-run wording).source ↗

The above are quotations of official pages or transcriptions of vendor-run benchmarks; this site does not endorse cross-model conclusions.
