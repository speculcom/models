Official spec facts (each with a source) as of

  • Context window　1,048,576 tokens (official Gemini 4 Argon cloud docs; Carbon / Neon also sit at the 1M tier)source ↗
  • Output limit　Argon has a unified 1,048,576 input/output limit; Carbon caps a single segment at 65,536 (streaming) / 32,768 (non-streaming) (official docs and launch page)source ↗
  • Pricing pointer　Billed by tier and usage; this site does not copy numbers — see the official pricing table.source ↗
  • Release dates　Argon GA on 2026-10-13; Carbon announced 10-28 and opened 11-10; Hydrogen launched 11-12; Neon on 2026-11-18 (official docs and blog)source ↗
  • Video generation　Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash: native 15-second video generation and multi-turn conversational editing, priced 30% below 1.1 for 720p and generally available on the Gemini API and Google Cloud from 2026-11-05; see the official launch page for the current rate (official launch page).source ↗
  • Family roles　Carbon takes the hardest long-horizon projects; Argon handles most software engineering and knowledge work; Neon does high-frequency complex work at 3x Carbon speed and 60% of its cost; Hydrogen handles fast everyday execution (official blog)source ↗

The above transcribe official docs and release pages; vendors may have changed specs since. For pricing, use the official pricing page linked below.
